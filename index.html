How to Play

  • Steer by dragging anywhere on the screen (floating joystick), with the arrow keys / WASD, or a gamepad.
  • Tilt steering (phones): turn it on in Settings, then tilt the phone toward where you want to go.
  • Fly through the gaps in the doors and dodge the bars.
  • Mine Mode: don't touch the mines drifting around the tunnel.
  • Grab the glowing hourglass to slow down time.
  • Pause with the button at the top left, Esc or P.
